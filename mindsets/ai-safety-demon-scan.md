# Demon Scan: AI Safety Establishment

> **Target**: AI safety/alignment research community and funding apparatus  
> **Framework**: Field Theory of Meaning (v0) — Demon-maintain operator  
> **Scan date**: 2026-01-18

---

## Primary Demon: "Technical Solutions to Political Problems"

### Invariant Claimed
"AI alignment is a technical problem solvable through research, requiring primarily technical expertise and technical funding."

### Observable Failures
- **Power concentration continues**: Same organizations accumulate AI capabilities regardless of safety research
- **Regulatory capture**: Safety researchers hired into capability labs, research priorities shift to lab needs  
- **Implementation gaps**: Safety research stays theoretical while deployment accelerates
- **Resource asymmetry**: Capability development vastly outfunded vs. safety research
- **Democratic exclusion**: Public has no meaningful input into AI development trajectories

### Cost Exports

**To researchers**:
- Career pressure to align with fundable technical angles
- Cognitive dissonance managing industry relationships vs. safety goals
- Responsibility for outcomes beyond their actual influence

**To public**:
- Excluded from decisions affecting their lives
- Blamed for "not understanding the technology" when opposing deployment
- Bear consequences of misaligned systems with no recourse

**To future generations**:
- Path-dependent technology deployment reducing future options
- Technical debt in safety approaches chosen for career/funding rather than effectiveness

### Control Mechanisms

**Gatekeeping**:
- Conference review prioritizes mathematical complexity over democratic engagement
- Funding flows through technical institutions rather than public accountability bodies
- "Not qualified" deflection of political/social questions

**Redefinition**:
- Political problems reframed as technical problems ("just solve alignment")
- Democratic input dismissed as "regulatory burden" rather than legitimacy requirement
- Corporate capture reframed as "industry partnership"

**Ritual maintenance**:
- Endless workshops on technical alignment while political alignment ignored
- Safety conferences that don't address power concentration
- "Responsible AI" initiatives that preserve status quo while signaling concern

### Demon Maintenance Cost

**Direct costs**:
- ~$100M+ annually in safety research that doesn't constrain deployment
- Academic-industrial complex maintaining technical framing
- PR apparatus managing public perception of safety efforts

**Opportunity costs**:
- Democratic deliberation mechanisms not developed
- Alternative development trajectories not explored
- Public expertise in technology governance not cultivated

### Distortion Profile

`D_demon ≈ 0.85`

**Authority confusion**: Unclear whether safety researchers work for public benefit or industry advancement  
**Causal reversal**: Safety research presented as causing safety rather than industry constraint  
**Metric gaming**: Papers published and conferences held substitute for actual safety outcomes  
**Responsibility diffusion**: No clear accountability for alignment failures

## Secondary Demon: "Inevitability of Current Trajectory"

### Invariant Claimed
"AI development follows technological imperatives; current trajectory is natural/inevitable."  

### Observable Failures
- **Historical contingency**: Multiple viable alternative paths (open source, public development, different architectures)
- **Policy choices**: Tax incentives, research funding, regulatory frameworks actively shape trajectory
- **Resource allocation**: Massive capital requirements not naturally distributed

### Cost Exports
- **To alternative researchers**: Difficulty securing funding for non-mainstream approaches
- **To smaller nations**: Technological dependence on a few dominant systems
- **To workers**: Displacement treated as natural rather than policy choice

### Control Mechanisms
- **Gatekeeping**: Academic journals, conferences favor approaches aligned with current trajectory
- **Redefinition**: Policy choices reframed as "keeping up with progress"
- **Ritual**: Constant discussion of "AI race" that treats competition as unchangeable

---

## Tertiary Demon: "Neutral Technical Expertise"

### Invariant Claimed
"AI researchers provide objective technical guidance free from political bias."

### Observable Failures
- **Funding sources**: Research priorities shaped by industry and military funding
- **Career incentives**: Advancement tied to relationships with capability labs
- **Framing biases**: Problems defined in ways amenable to current technical approaches

### Cost Exports
- **To marginalized communities**: Their concerns dismissed as "non-technical"  
- **To public**: False confidence in neutral expertise while biased decisions made
- **To researchers**: Cognitive overhead maintaining neutrality pose while navigating conflicts

## Intervention Points (Demon Disruption)

### High-leverage termination conditions

1. **Alternative funding structures**: Public research funding not routed through industry-adjacent institutions
2. **Democratic technology assessment**: Public bodies with actual decision authority over AI deployment  
3. **Transparency requirements**: Open access to safety research, conflicts of interest disclosure
4. **Alternative metrics**: Success measured by democratic input and deployment constraint rather than technical outputs

### Specific counter-rituals

**Replace**: Technical alignment conferences  
**With**: Public deliberation forums on AI governance with technical translation  

**Replace**: Industry-academic partnerships  
**With**: Public research institutions with independence guarantees  

**Replace**: "Responsible AI" voluntary frameworks  
**With**: Binding regulatory frameworks with democratic oversight  

### Resistance prediction
- "Technical people need to lead on technical problems" (authority preservation)
- "Regulation will stifle innovation" (inevitability preservation)
- "Public doesn't understand AI" (neutrality preservation)

## Measurement Protocol

**Demon persistence indicators**:
- % of safety researchers funded by capability labs
- Ratio of technical papers to governance mechanisms in safety conferences
- Public exclusion from AI development decisions (measure via policy influence)

**Termination indicators**:
- Alternative research funding flows operational
- Public bodies with AI deployment authority
- Safety research influencing actual deployment constraints

**Meta-demon watch**:
- This analysis itself being dismissed as "not technical enough"
- Safety community developing immune response to political analysis
- Counter-demon systems that preserve underlying power structure
