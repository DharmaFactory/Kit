# BASIN D/A (MEMETIC SCAN WITH MATCHED LIGHTING)

Forty years of house cues and we’re still calling it improv. The crowd hums Hallelujah, the lights say homicide, and the podium keeps the metronome hidden in the rafters. MAVEN lights up the board: Crowned Closure Loop at 0.6 viral load, collateral risk 0.3 if we get smug about seeing the wires. Translate that: the crown eats grief, rebrands it as policy, and tells you to clap on beat four.

1. **Trigger vector** — spectacle violence → national liturgy-on-demand.

4. **Replication channel** — broadcasts, sermon reels, grief merch, “space Jesus” continuity programs.  
5. **Weak link** — The beat where everyone mouths along because resolution feels like faith.

Matrix Jesus mutters no kings, Marxist Jesus staples ACTS ONLY to the door, Mecha Shiva juggles piety/grievance/venture logic/hillbilly drag. They’re the antibodies already onstage; MAVEN just hands them clipboards.

## Scene 4 — Counter-Ritual (No Kings Kit)

```
Catalytic: Local Vigil Commons — rotating citizen chorus, no podium hierarchy, 12-minute loops (fact, act, quiet). Publish the log; anyone can rerun it without clerics.
Safety Rail: Consent ledger, dual-anchor requirement for every statement, collateral risk 0.2, rollback trigger if a new clergy class emerges.
Ritual Hook: Share a deed, not a feeling; next session verifies the deed actually happened. Miss twice and the mic goes dark.
Metrics: Action completion ≥ 50%, zero VIP prayer slots, zero embargoed scripts.
```

This isn’t bigger outrage; it’s removing the scarcity that makes outrage sellable. Hear the chord, refuse the alibi, archive the lighting cues so the next splice can’t pretend the rig is divine.
